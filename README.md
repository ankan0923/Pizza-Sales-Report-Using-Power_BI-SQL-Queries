# Pizza Sales Report
## Summary
This project demonstrates how  power bi p-query  Data processing, cleaning, dax function can be applied to sales Management. also parallelly use and run SQL queries to comparing data By modeling sales by categoty, pizza size and monthly variations, shop owner can predict with sales targets.These insights enable better decision‑making in compensation planning.

## Objectives
To visulize trends and profitability across Total Orders vs by month, day and Total Revenue by category,pizza size etc. Evaluate sales performance across pizzas and formats to identify high‑value products, weak performers, demand patterns, and concrete opportunities to increase revenue and efficiency. To analyze and comparing with data parallelly run queries in SQL on PostgeSQL.  
## Methodology
Data Collection: Sales data aggregated for 2015, covering revenue, orders, and pizza categories.
Data Cleaning: analyze the data replace value in power query pizza size.
Data Engineering: create measure for aggregation and KPI(), create calculated columns(from date take out day & montly name, number etc),
Implement Time Intelligence with DAX functions (WEEKDAY,DATE UPPERCASE) for period comparisons,
Optimize and validate: test with sample filters, check performance, and document logic. 
## Key Insights
Revenue concentration: Chicken variants (Thai, Barbecue, California) are the top revenue drivers.
Volume leader: Classic Deluxe leads in quantity and orders, making it essential for retention and volume sales.
Size opportunity: Large pizzas account for the largest revenue share (~46%); upselling size is a high‑impact lever.
Underperformers: Brie Carre consistently ranks lowest across revenue, quantity, and orders — prime candidate for recipe change, repositioning, or removal.
Demand timing: Orders peak on Fridays and Saturdays; July is the strongest month while October is weakest — use this for staffing and timed promotions.
Category balance: Classic and Supreme together form the majority of revenue; Chicken is close behind, suggesting a three‑pillar product focus (Classic, Supreme, Chicken).
## Recomendations
Promote high‑margin chicken pizzas with targeted bundles and featured placement.
Upsell large sizes at checkout and in combos to increase AOV.
Test revival tactics (discounts, recipe tweak, repositioning) for Brie Carre and other low performers for a limited period; if no lift, remove.
Align operations to weekend peaks and run off‑peak promotions in weak months.
